# Visibility lives on the account

An account is Household or Personal. That's fixed when it is created, and its transactions inherit it. A personal account belongs to its member, not the household, so it goes with them when they leave or move.

A transfer only links accounts of the same visibility. Money crossing between personal and household is household income or spending, so allowances show up as household spending. Moving a transaction across the boundary is recorded as removing it from one account and adding it to the other. That way every event is visible to everyone whose view it changes.

We rejected per-transaction visibility, which the previous app used. It allowed personal entries on household accounts, which made balances unexplainable to other members. It also left household records pointing at accounts a departed member had taken with them.
